Interaction guide and model limits

Touch or click the patient, mattress, backrest, bed frame, rails, camera, monitor, IV stand, service panel, wall, window, or floor to inspect it. Use the controls for precise changes. Dragging the patient to the floor and holding for 0.85 seconds selects standing; releasing early produces a gravity-driven fall. Keyboard and touch users can also use the patient buttons.

This is a portfolio reconstruction of the IoT bed concept, not a clinical system. Camera events come from scene pose rules, not a trained vision model. Vital signs are synthetic scenario signals. Falls trigger a scripted stress response with a higher pulse and breathing rate and a small oxygen change. Returning to bed allows gradual recovery. These responses do not predict injury or actual patient physiology. Sensors are assumed to remain attached; movement reduces the displayed signal quality. Geometry, motion, and thresholds are illustrative. No injury, diagnosis, treatment, or hardware safety claim is made.
