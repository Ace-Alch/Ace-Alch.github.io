Model, controls & engineering details

What this lab calculates

Utility active power is the residual after PV, BESS, CHP and demand. Both charge and export reverse the animated flow. The CHP plane shows setpoint magnitude and reference power factor. It is not a network phasor solution.

Migration work

Map source components and reference directions, stabilize PV MPPT, tune converter current loops, check CHP sign and rate limits, and validate PCC balance. Loads 1–5 are represented by one total-demand control here.

Model boundary

No load flow, line loss, voltage dynamics, battery energy limit or protection model is solved. BESS2 is excluded because its rating was not supplied. Numerical ratings are retained from the documented migration project.
