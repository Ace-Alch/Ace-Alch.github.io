Model, controls & engineering details

What changes on the bench

Duty sets the on-time fraction. Frequency changes period and time scale. Resistance changes load current and power. Bench power turns all electrical output off. MQTT only affects telemetry.

Calculated, not measured

The ideal resistor has no inductance. ADC conversion assumes a 0–5 A range and 12-bit resolution. This is not the thesis constant-power load, MPPT converter or DAB plant. No hardware measurements are invented.

Prototype architecture

The concept separates local MCU control, sensing, relay actuation and Raspberry Pi communication. The live circuit explains those interfaces. It is not a PCB manufacturing or wiring document.
