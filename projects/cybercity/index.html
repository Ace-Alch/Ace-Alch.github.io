Model, controls & engineering details

Control architecture

PV uses P&O MPPT. A PI-controlled DAB links each 3S battery to its 12 V bus. Local MCUs act first. Reserve thresholds determine donor eligibility. Main control can take ownership after loss of the MG2 heartbeat.

DAB PI: Kp 0.25, Ki 8.0, deadband 0.01. Generator RPM PI: Kp 220, Ki 1800. These are thesis controller settings; this browser does not solve those loops.

Read the experiment

Select S3 and move the timeline past 6 seconds to see donor support. Disable sharing and compare. In S5, compare Main takeover on and off. In S6, generator capacity is limited and load priority matters.

The live model conserves allocated power. Voltage is an estimate from unmet load. It excludes SPS switching, converter losses and transient dynamics.

Thesis validation

MATLAB R2025b, Specialized Power Systems, discrete powergui at 5 μs; 20-second runs. Reported S1: 100% voltage compliance and 99.73% load served. These are separate thesis results, not outputs from this browser.

The example loads, generation and detection delay above are demonstrator parameters. Exact recreation needs the original .slx model and its source data.
