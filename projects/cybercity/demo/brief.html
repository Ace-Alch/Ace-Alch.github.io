Controller and utility update

The archived model contains the MATLAB functions LocalMCU_New, MainMCU_Final and the DAQ evaluator. The browser uses their 34 W local adequacy cap, request hysteresis, donor reserve thresholds, and startup relay holdoff. The relay input uses the MATLAB codes 0 for open, 1 for closed, and 2 for automatic. The switching and dispatch shown in the browser run at a 50 ms step. Published KPIs and the separate Simulink solver remain the scientific reference.

The pylons show the relay and generator paths at city scale. The central water pump is an illustrative MG2 load. Its served power drives the four-district water network and gates downstream water effects. This adds a game dependency while keeping the original electrical topology and baseline loads.

Implementation prompt

Build The Living Grid

Turn the CyberCity thesis demo into a cinematic and playable city. Preserve one electrical state across Explore, Engineering Sandbox, and Resilience Challenges. Let visitors approach from the air, walk up to equipment, operate it, and see how the city responds.

World and play

Design recognizable hospital, industrial, civic, and residential districts. Give each a reason to visit at street level. Use licensed, optimized models. Add traffic and pedestrians, vegetation, changing skies, night lighting, and optional audio. Drive lamps, pump flow, and factory equipment from delivered power. Make every electrical action readable in the world, the single-line diagram, and the diagnostics.

Thesis traceability

Use the published thesis and archived Simulink file as separate sources. Follow the 12 V architecture, independent generator branches, shared relay bus, district priorities, and six original 20-second schedules. Compare the published 11.6–12.8 V KPI band with the 11–13 V band in the archived model. Use the thesis band for the published comparison and identify the archive's separate recovery rule in technical documentation.

Map Ruser to load resistance, S to irradiance, TempC to PV temperature, SW1–SW4 to battery, PV, demand and generator feeder, and RPi_SW to local MCU availability. Show Vbus, SoC, source and load power, cmdRelay, reqFlag, and mainCmd. Identify visual load shares and any simplified solver assumptions. Use raw Simulink traces for a validated replay only when the traces are available.
