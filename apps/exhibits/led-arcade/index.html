A spaceship game on a physical RGB panel. The PYNQ-Z1 runs the game with C, ARM assembly, and timer interrupts.

The physical controls

BTN0 Move right   BTN2 Move left

BTN1 Fire   BTN3 Restart

Two timer paths

800 Hz updates the ship rows. 10 Hz advances the game logic.
