Model before hardware

CyberCity studies a DC energy architecture for a laboratory smart-city platform. The thesis model and lab prototype work have separate scopes.

Local and supervisory decisions

Local control manages each microgrid. Supervisory logic coordinates energy sharing and degraded operation.

Scenario-based evaluation

Six scenarios examine baseline operation, stressed receivers, donor rejection, local control failure and a combined case. Acceptance targets are separate from measured outcomes.
